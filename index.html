A LOOK BEHIND THE DEMO

Useful wording.
Transparent sources.

YoCV is an independent student concept for Yoma. It helps young people review how their experience appears in a CV.

Working now

A complete, grounded CV journey

Role selection, live AI suggestions when a model is configured (otherwise local templates, always labelled), server-side grounding checks that reject sentences not supported by your own words, skills matched to Yoma’s public skill taxonomy, learning opportunities from Yoma’s public API, individual claim review, source records, CV preview and downloads.

Samples and limits

Evidence and identity

The credential is a representative sample based on Yoma’s wallet credential model, not an issued credential. Selected files stay on your device; this prototype records their names and your descriptions only. There is no Yoma sign-in, so nothing here is your own Yoma data.

Proposed Yoma integration

Enter through Yo-ID’s existing CV placeholder. With a Yoma-issued sign-in client and the user’s consent, retrieve profile, verified skills and wallet credentials in place of the sample. Keep the generation service and its grounding checks server-side with an access-controlled claim review record. Document verification and job submission remain future work.

Approving a sentence does not verify it. An issuer’s credential supports only the achievement it actually records. No UNICEF or Yoma endorsement is implied.
