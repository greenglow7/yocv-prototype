About this demo

YoCV is an independent student concept for Yoma. It helps young people review how their experience appears in a CV.

What works now

  • Role selection and a reviewed CV draft.
  • Live AI suggestions when a model is configured, otherwise local templates, always labelled.
  • Server-side grounding checks that reject sentences not supported by your own words.
  • Skills matched to Yoma’s public skill taxonomy and learning opportunities from Yoma’s public API.
  • Individual claim review, source records, CV preview and downloads.

What is sample data

  • The credential is a representative sample based on Yoma’s wallet credential model, not an issued credential.
  • Selected files stay on your device. This prototype records their names and your descriptions only.
  • There is no Yoma sign-in, so nothing here is your own Yoma data.

Proposed Yoma integration

Enter through Yo-ID’s existing CV placeholder. With a Yoma-issued sign-in client and the user’s consent, retrieve profile, verified skills and wallet credentials in place of the sample. Keep the generation service and its grounding checks server-side with an access-controlled claim review record. Document verification and job submission remain future work.

Approving a sentence does not verify it. An issuer’s credential supports only the achievement it actually records. No UNICEF or Yoma endorsement is implied.
