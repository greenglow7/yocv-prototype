About this demo

YoCV is an independent student concept for Yoma. It turns everyday work into a first CV, and the person reviews every line before it goes on.

What works now

  • Reading a Yoma record: completed opportunities, wallet credentials and self-attested skills, each ticked to include and never edited.
  • A short interview about up to three pieces of work, one question per screen on a phone.
  • Live AI suggestions when a model is configured, otherwise template wording, always labelled. Every suggestion is checked against the person’s own answers.
  • Skills labelled by the evidence behind them: confirmed from Yoma, vouched on YoCard, or self-stated.
  • Contact details stay for the CV only. Names, phone numbers, emails and towns are never sent to a model.

What is sample data

  • The accounts are fictional sample personas. Their credentials follow Yoma’s wallet model and were not issued by Yoma.
  • There is no Yoma sign-in, so nothing here is your own Yoma data unless the server holds a Yoma access token.
  • A photo and the other optional details stay in this browser.

Proposed Yoma integration

Enter through Yo-ID’s existing CV placeholder. With a Yoma-issued sign-in client and the user’s consent, read the profile, completions, skills and wallet credentials in place of the sample. Keep the generation service and its checks behind one server-side interface, with an access-controlled evidence record. Document verification and job submission remain future work.

Approving a sentence does not verify it. An issuer’s credential supports only the achievement it actually records. Not operated by Yoma or UNICEF. No UNICEF or Yoma endorsement is implied.
