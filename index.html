YoCV is an independent student concept for Yoma. It helps young people review how their experience appears in a CV.

Working now

A complete CV journey

Role selection, local sample suggestions, individual claim review, source records, CV preview and downloads.

Demonstrated with samples

Evidence and generation

Credentials are synthetic. The generator uses local rules and templates, with no live AI call. Selected files stay on your device; this prototype records their names and your descriptions only.

Proposed Yoma integration

Enter through Yo-ID’s existing CV placeholder or an opportunity. With consent, retrieve profile, skills and credential details. Add a server-side model service and an access-controlled claim review record. Live Yoma sign-in, AI, document verification and job submission are future work.

Approving a sentence does not verify it. An issuer’s credential supports only the achievement it actually records. No UNICEF or Yoma endorsement is implied.
