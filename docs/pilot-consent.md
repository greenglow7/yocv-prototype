# YoVouch pilot consent text

Two short texts in plain words: one for workers, one for customers. They must be signed off by the consultant's institution before the first real request (spec section 15). No Yoma approval exists for this pilot.

Institution sign-off: name ____________ role ____________ date ____________

Read each text aloud or give it to the person to read. Answer every question before anything is recorded. A person can say no at any point, and saying no changes nothing for them.

## For workers

This is a student study. It is not run by Yoma or UNICEF. Yoma has not approved it. The only approval is from ____________ (the institution).

You will use a prototype called YoVouch. It lets past customers say they used your work. Their words appear on your YoCard, a web page with your work details.

What we store:

- Your card, as you published it, with the time it was read from your sample or Yoma account.
- The vouches your customers make, with the date and how they were made.
- Clips of your voice, only if you record one.
- A log of events, such as "a request link was made". It holds ids and times only. It holds no names and no phone numbers.

What we do not store:

- Your gender. If you tell us your gender, it goes on the study sheet kept by the student, not on your card or in any record.
- Any phone number of yours or of your customers in readable form.

You can stop at any time. You can withdraw your card at any time, and every vouch on it goes with it. Tell the student, or use the withdraw button on your card.

YoVouch never sends a message for you. You forward each request from your own phone, if you want to.

The day after you make request links, the student will ask you one question on WhatsApp: "Did you send the request? To how many people?" Two weeks later the student will ask if any reply led to contact, a job or a refusal. You do not have to answer.

Every clip is deleted at the end of the pilot.

## For customers

A worker asked you to say you used their work. This is part of a student study. It is not run by Yoma or UNICEF. Yoma has not approved it.

What we store:

- What you tap and the few words you type or say, on the worker's card.
- Your first name, if you give it.
- A keyed hash of your phone number. This is a scrambled code made with a secret key. It lets us stop one number vouching too often. Nobody can read your number back from it.
- The last three digits of your number, only if you choose to show them.
- A clip of your voice, only if you record one and tick Attach. If you speak your words, the written text of what you said is kept with the vouch.

Your words and clip appear on the card only after the worker reads them and taps Show. Until then the card shows only what you tapped.

Your number is not confirmed. There is no SMS service in this pilot, so every vouch carries the label `Number typed by the voucher, not confirmed (demo)`. This is a demo-fallback vouch, and the card says so.

Who can see this: anyone with the link to the worker's card.

You can withdraw your vouch at any time with the withdraw link you get at the end. You can withdraw it at once or quietly over the next three days.

Every clip is deleted at the end of the pilot. Other records are kept for the pilot period only.

You must be 18 or older to take part.
