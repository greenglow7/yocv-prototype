Talent Finder

Partners search an anonymised pool of verified young people by profile and notify the ones who match.

1Search2Results3Profile4Notify5Young person

Five static screens for GenU's New Ideas section, on the partner and operations side of Yoma. Plain HTML in the Yo-ID look, built from the YoCV prototype's tokens. No backend, no search, no AI. The profiles are fictional; the first one is derived from a YoCV sample persona.

The flow, start to finish

  1. 1SearchPartner. Filter the opt-in pool by Yoma skills, evidence level, learning completed, region and availability.
  2. 2ResultsPartner. Anonymised cards with an evidence label on every skill. Fewer than 5 matches shows no list.
  3. 3Anonymised profilePartner. Confirmed from Yoma, vouched for by other people, self-declared: the YoCV employer check groups.
  4. 4NotifyPartner. Pick a live Yoma listing and a short message, and preview what the young person receives.
  5. 5The notice in Yo-IDYoung person, on a phone. Share my CV hands over the YoCV CV check link. Not interested shows the partner only "no reply".

The time and cost of not having it

A partner fills an entry-level role today by posting, waiting and screening unverified applications by hand. Verified young people who never see the post are not found at all.

Privacy rules shown on the screens

  • Opt-in pool only
  • No contact details until the young person shares
  • No list below 5 matches
  • No vouch counts, no voucher names
  • No export of the pool
